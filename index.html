OhMyVacay

An Android app for planning and tracking your trips

About the App

OhMyVacay is an Android app that lets you create an account, log in, and manage a personal list of vacations — each with its own lodging details, travel dates, and optional excursions. Every account only ever sees its own data, and everything is stored locally on the device, so no internet connection or external server is required.
